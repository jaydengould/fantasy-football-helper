**Yahoo gives you back-to-back picks all draft** — 19/22, 39/42, 59/62, 79/82,
99/102, 119/122, 139/142. Two or three picks apart, every round from the second.
That is the slot-2 wheel and it changes how you play:

- **You can take a pair.** Two RBs, or the QB you want plus the WR you were
  worried about. Nobody gets between them.
- **Never reach at the front half of a pair.** If two players you like are both
  there, take the one more likely to go — you get the other 3 picks later.
- The cost is a 17-pick gap between 2 and 19. Your first pick is nearly a
  round-and-a-half alone.

### Sleeper-specific traps

- **TE ADP is inflated ~20 picks.** Sleeper's `adp_ppr` folds in TE-premium
  leagues. Yours is not one. **Treat every TE SURV number as too pessimistic** —
  they will last longer than the board says.
- **SURV is an ordering, not a probability.** Measured on 540 real picks: the
  model says 0–20% and roughly a third survive. It is right about *who* is more
  likely to last, wrong about *how likely*. Never read it as a percentage.
- **The board leans toward reaching**, because it understates survival. When it
  is close, waiting is cheaper than it looks.
- **Late rounds will push TEs at you.** TE has the shallowest replacement, so
  static VBD floats them once your starters are full. Those rows carry a `BENCH`
  flag, which is the tool saying *trust yourself here*. Take upside, not TE4.
- **Don't take K/DEF before the last two rounds.** Nothing in the model knows
  better than you do here.

### 3. Draft RBs early

The top 13 of your Yahoo board is **10 RBs**. Half PPR takes receptions off the
WRs, and 10 teams means replacement is only RB30 — so the elite backs gain
against the pool. Your pick 2 is Gibbs or Bijan, whichever is left; both sources

### 4. Yahoo scoring oddities

- **INT −2** (Sleeper is −1). Turnover-prone QBs cost double here.
- **No FG-miss penalty** (Sleeper has −1). Kickers are marginally safer; still
  don't draft one early.
- **Return yards and return TDs score, and our projections contain none of them.**
  Sleeper's data carries no return stats at all, so any return specialist is
  worth *more* than any number you have. Known blind spot — it is the one place
  to override on your own read.
